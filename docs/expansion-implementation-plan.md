# Yardly expansion implementation plan

Source: approved `Yardly Expansion Proposal`, dated October 10, 2026. This plan keeps the expansion separate from the existing instant outdoor-space reservation and Stripe work.

## Bounded delivery sequence

1. **Provider foundation (complete):** additive schema/RLS for provider profiles and staff review; provider submission UI; staff review queue; public services/rentals directory; validation and browser/database coverage.
2. **Requests and quotes (complete except notification delivery):** service/equipment request records, quote terms, participant messages, and guarded status transitions. No reservation or payment coupling. Notification delivery remains a launch dependency because no email/SMS provider or product rule is approved.
3. **Churches (workflow complete; launch content pending):** church-owned approval workflow, photo-permission record, staff review, event requests and shareable approved listing page.
4. **Community directory (workflow complete; launch content pending):** curated entries, official-source records, seven-day freshness gate, staff recheck queue and official-link handoff. Source-specific automation remains excluded until source/access approval.
5. **Booking assistance (human-reviewed workflow complete; AI generation and real submissions excluded):** intake, up to three staff-reviewed suggestions, up to two sequential application drafts, per-application customer approval, two-follow-up limit, and evidence-based confirmation. The ten-business-day calculation excludes weekends; an approved timezone and holiday calendar remain launch gates.
6. **Fee policy and launch:** the proposed `$49` outcomes are stored only as noncharging previews and the schema prevents a charged state. A separate explicit decision remains required before billing or customer-facing payment terms. Complete Tim's five-feature walkthrough, privacy/notification checks, non-production migration validation, preview QA, real onboarding/content, and publication scope.

## First-slice acceptance criteria

- A signed-in user can create a photographer, approved event-professional, or equipment-rental profile as a draft and submit it for review.
- Submitted profiles cannot be edited until staff returns them to draft/rejects them; approval metadata is recorded.
- Only explicitly assigned marketplace staff can see and decide the review queue.
- Anonymous users can read only approved profiles; owners can read their own profiles in every state.
- Public cards expose provider name, samples, service area, offerings, starting price, and rental fulfillment details where applicable.
- Loading, empty, error, validation, submitted and approved states are clear and keyboard accessible at mobile and desktop widths.
- No sample provider appears as live inventory and no payment, booking, credential, dependency-manifest, native, or production configuration changes are made.

## Request-and-quote acceptance criteria

- A signed-in customer can request a reviewed provider with date, location, hours, guest count and needs; equipment requests also require item quantities and may include options.
- Creating a request does not create a quote, reservation, inventory hold or payment record.
- Only the provider owner can quote a request, with full price, deposit, inclusions and cancellation terms.
- Only the customer can accept the quote, and acceptance requires an explicit acknowledgment that payment is handled directly with the provider.
- Only the provider can confirm, and only after customer acceptance and direct-payment acknowledgment. Decline and close transitions remain explicit.
- Customer and provider can read and send private request messages; unrelated users cannot read or modify the workflow.
- UI labels match the approved states: Waiting for a reply, Quoted, Confirmed, Declined and Closed.
- No Stripe call, combined checkout, inventory reservation, Yardly fee, or `$49` booking-help charge is introduced.
